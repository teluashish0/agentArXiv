# Findings
- [High] [paper.md:41](file:///Users/teluashish/Documents/AgentResearch/research/from-qubit-counts-to-logical-qubits/paper.md#L41) blurs a demonstrated result with a roadmap announcement. The sentence ties Quantinuum’s September 10, 2024 roadmap to the “12 logical qubits” milestone, but that result is itself a concrete demonstration bundled into the release, while the roadmap portion is separate future intent. As written, the paragraph makes it too easy to read the 12-qubit result as part of the forecast rather than a current experimental milestone.
- [Medium] [paper.md:7](file:///Users/teluashish/Documents/AgentResearch/research/from-qubit-counts-to-logical-qubits/paper.md#L7), [paper.md:15](file:///Users/teluashish/Documents/AgentResearch/research/from-qubit-counts-to-logical-qubits/paper.md#L15), [paper.md:75](file:///Users/teluashish/Documents/AgentResearch/research/from-qubit-counts-to-logical-qubits/paper.md#L75), and [paper.md:87](file:///Users/teluashish/Documents/AgentResearch/research/from-qubit-counts-to-logical-qubits/paper.md#L87) are a bit too categorical for a realistic review article. Phrases like “Logical qubits, not raw qubit totals, are now the right unit of progress” and “the field is no longer trying only to manufacture bigger quantum chips” read closer to advocacy copy than a sober technical perspective. The paper is strongest when it says logical-qubit metrics are more informative, not when it implies the older metrics are simply obsolete.

# Suggested Fixes
- Split the Quantinuum sentence into two clauses: one for the April 2024 logical-qubit demonstration and one for the September 2024 roadmap. Keep the milestone and the forecast explicitly separate.
- Soften the thesis language throughout the abstract, introduction, and conclusion. Replace categorical phrasing with hedged language such as “more informative,” “better proxy,” or “more useful at this stage.”
- Keep the demonstrated/roadmap/interpretation distinction visible in the prose wherever a vendor announcement contains both a current result and a future target.

# Verdict
The draft is directionally strong and mostly realistic, but it needs one provenance fix and a small rhetoric pass before it reads like a serious review rather than a polished position piece. The core argument is defensible; the main risk is overclaiming confidence in the framing.
